### Heat Issues & Observations
*   **Group 1:** Discussed various factors contributing to heat in the residential areas:
    *   *Surface Reflection:* Heat is intensified by reflections from glass windows/mirrors and concrete surfaces in front of houses.
    *   *Canal Humidity:* When the local canal (คลอง) heats up, it causes a significant increase in uncomfortable humidity.
    *   *Building Shadows (Pros & Cons):* Large buildings can cast shadows that block direct sunlight, which helps lower the temperature in some community areas. However, depending on the layout, it can sometimes have the opposite effect (e.g., blocking natural wind flow or trapping heat). 

*   **Ruam Pattana Community (ชุมชนร่วมพัฒนา):** Despite currently having many trees, the area is becoming increasingly densely populated. Residents expressed concerns that they might soon lose the cool and shaded environment they traditionally enjoyed.

## 4. Chatbot Feedback

### What Worked Well (Pros)
*   **Incentives:** Using giveaways and activities to incentivize participation proved to be highly effective.

### What Didn't Work (Cons/Issues)
*   **Poor User Experience (Elderly Focus):** Elderly users reported that the chatbot is difficult to use. They are particularly uncomfortable with the LINE Flex Message (bubble) UI. The current design feels too "system-centric" rather than user-friendly.
*   **Technological Barrier:** Many people feel frustrated and alienated by the technology. Some even struggle with basic digital tasks, such as adding the LINE Official Account as a friend.
*   **Multiple Reports Friction:** Users complained about the process of reporting multiple issues at the same time. Having to go through the entire reporting flow repeatedly for each individual problem causes significant frustration.
### What's Next (Action Items)
*   **AI Integration:** Exploring ways to implement AI features into the chatbot to replace or enhance the current rule-based system.
